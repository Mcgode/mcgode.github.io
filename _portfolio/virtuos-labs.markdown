I joined Virtuos Labs as a Software Engineer, with a specialty in graphics programming.
Virtuos is a game outsourcing studio,  with Virtuos Labs being its engineering division, which also handles the 
developing of internal technologies to propose to their clients. 

I have been tasked with the integration of on such technologies, named Hi-Res Vision. Its goal is to integrate 
virtualized geometry with support for non-uniform LOD geometry, similar to Unreal Engine's Nanite. 
You can find more info in their [GDC talk](https://gdcvault.com/play/1034621/Maximizing-Graphics-Performance-with-Flexible)
